[physical_disabilities_supported]
  - Mobility impairments (e.g., wheelchair/scooter use, difficulty walking or climbing stairs, temporary injuries)
  - Dexterity limitations (e.g., arthritis, repetitive strain, hand/arm impairments)
  - Vision and hearing impairments (e.g., low vision, blindness, partial to total hearing loss)
  - Chronic health conditions affecting academic participation
  - Temporary or episodic conditions (e.g., post-surgery recovery, concussion, fractures)

[mental_disabilities_supported]
  - anxiety disorders (Generalized Anxiety Disorder, Social Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder)
  - mood disorders (Major Depressive Disorder, Bipolar Disorder), thought disorders (Schizophrenia)
  - eating disorders
  - personality disorders
  - learning disabilities
  - attention disorders (ADD/ADHD)

  - Extra time, breaks, and quiet rooms for exams
  - Alternate formats: audio, Braille, large print, PDFs
  - Assistive tech and adaptive software support
  - Note-taking and lecture recording options
  - Personalized accommodation plans each term
  - Support for physical and temporary conditions
  - Temporary accommodations available
  - Accessible campus: ramps, elevators, washrooms, housing
  - Confidential service; diagnosis disclosure not required

[osap_eligibility]
  - Laurentian University, situated in Sudbury, Ontario, adheres to the provincial guidelines for the Ontario Student Assistance Program (OSAP). This means the core eligibility criteria for OSAP funding are the same for students at Laurentian as they are for students at any other publicly funded post-secondary institution in Ontario.
  - General OSAP Eligibility
  - To be eligible for OSAP at Laurentian University, you must typically meet the following requirements:
  - Ontario Resident: You must be an Ontario resident. This is generally defined by having lived in Ontario for at least 12 consecutive months, not including time spent as a full-time post-secondary student.
  - Citizenship/Status: You must be a Canadian citizen, Permanent Resident of Canada, or a Protected Person. International students are not eligible for OSAP.
  - Approved Program of Study: You must be enrolled in an OSAP-approved program that leads to a degree, diploma, or certificate. Most undergraduate, Master's, and Doctoral programs at Laurentian are approved. If you are in a non-degree program or certain specialized certificate programs, it's best to confirm eligibility with Laurentian's Student Awards Office.
  - Eligible Course Load: Your registration must meet the minimum requirements for either full-time or part-time OSAP.
  - Good Standing: You must not be in default on any previous OSAP loans or have any restrictions on your OSAP account.
  - Satisfactory Academic Progress: You need to maintain satisfactory academic progress in your program of study.
  - Financial Need: Your application will be assessed based on your and your family's (if applicable) financial situation to determine your financial need.
  - Lifetime Funding Limit: You must not have exceeded the lifetime limit of student loan funding (typically 340 weeks for most students, 400 weeks for doctoral students, or up to 520 weeks for students with a permanent disability).
  - First-Time Applicant Module: If you are a full-time or micro-credential student making your first OSAP application for the 2024–2025 or 2025–2026 school year, you will be required to complete an interactive financial literacy module.
