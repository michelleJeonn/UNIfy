Western University (Main Campus)
================================

[physical_disabilities_supported]
  - Mobility impairments: wheelchair or scooter users, difficulty walking/climbing stairs, temporary injuries
  - Dexterity limitations: arthritis, repetitive strain injuries, issues with fine motor skills
  - Sensory impairments: vision loss (low vision to blindness), hearing impairment
  - Chronic medical conditions: MS, asthma, chronic fatigue, etc.
  - Temporary physical conditions: concussion, post-surgery recovery, fractures

[mental_disabilities_supported]
  - anxiety disorders (Generalized Anxiety Disorder, Social Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder)
  - mood disorders (Major Depressive Disorder, Bipolar Disorder), thought disorders (Schizophrenia)
  - eating disorders
  - personality disorders
  - learning disabilities
  - attention disorders (ADD/ADHD)

[physical_accommodations]
  - Personalized accommodation plans for students with disabilities
  - Exam support: extra time, breaks, private/quiet rooms
  - Accessible formats: audio, Braille, large print, PDFs
  - Assistive technology support: software and hardware tools
  - ASL interpretation and real-time captioning offered
  - Peer note-taking and lecture recording available
  - Interim accommodations available during temporary conditions
  - Accessible campus features: parking, ramps, and buildings
  - Confidential coordination with faculty to implement accommodations

[mental_accommodations]
  - Personalized accommodation plans for students with disabilities
  - Exam support: extra time, breaks, private/quiet rooms
  - Accessible formats: audio, large print, PDFs
  - Assistive technology support: software and hardware tools
  - Peer note-taking and lecture recording available
  - Interim accommodations available during temporary conditions
  - Confidential coordination with faculty to implement accommodations

[documentation_requirements]
  - Acquired Brain Injury & Concussion:
  - Certificate of Acquired Brain Injury & Concussion Disability (PDF)
  - Recent medical documentation from an appropriate regulated health care practitioner (e.g., an emergency room physician, sports medicine, concussion specialist, family doctor, and/or neurologist) that outlines the functional impacts of your disability and your accommodation needs.
  - Psycho-educational Assessment
  - Certificate of ADHD Disability (PDF)
  - Certificate of Hearing Disability (PDF)
  - Documentation may take the form of a detailed letter from an appropriate health care practitioner (e.g. Audiologist, Otolaryngologist (ENT) or family doctor) that outlines the functional impacts of your disability and your accommodation needs.
  - Learning Disabilities:
  - Certificate of Medical Disability (PDF)
  - Documentation may take the form of a detailed letter from an appropriate health care practitioner (e.g. family doctor. specialist physician nd/or nurse practitioner) that outlines the functional impacts of your disability and your accommodation needs.
  - Mental Health:
  - Certificate of Mental Health (PDF)
  - Documentation may take the form of a detailed letter from an appropriate health care practitioner (e.g. psychiatrist, psychologist, family doctor and/or nurse practitioner) that outlines the functional impacts of your disability and your accommodation needs.
  - Physical/Mobility:
  - Certificate of Physical Mobility (PDF)
  - Documentation may take the form of a detailed letter from an appropriate health care practitioner (e.g. an emergency room physician, family doctor, and/or neurologist) that outlines the functional impacts of your disability and your accommodation needs.
  - Certificate of Vision Disability (PDF)
  - Documentation may take the form of a detailed letter from an appropriate health care practitioner (e.g. Ophthalmologist, Optometrist and/or family doctor) that outlines the functional impacts of your disability and your accommodation needs.

[registration_process]
  - Complete our online registration form.
  - Provide supporting documentation that verifies your disability. Find information about documentation here.
  - After we have reviewed your Primary Documentation for Registration you will be invited to attend an Intake & Onboarding appointment.
  - Meet with your Accessible Education Facilitator to discuss your eligibility for accommodations during your Western career.

[osap_eligibility]
  - Residency Requirements
  - OSAP is open to Ontario residents who are:
  - Canadian Citizen
  - Permanent Resident
  - Protected Person (Protected Person Status Document is required)
  - Course Load Requirements
  - In order to be considered a full-time student for the purpose of receiving OSAP funding you must meet one of the following criteria:
  - Undergraduate students must be fully-registered student in at least 60% of a full-time course load per term (the equivalent of at least three half courses per term)
  - Undergraduate students who have a permanent disability are eligible if enrolled in at least 40% of a full-time course load per term (the equivalent of at least two half courses per term)
  - All full-time graduate students are assessed for OSAP based on a 100% course load. Part-time students are considered to be registered in a 50% course load. Only graduate students who self-identify as having a permanent disability are eligible for full-time OSAP at this course load.
