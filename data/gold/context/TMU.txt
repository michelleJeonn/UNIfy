TMU
===

[physical_disabilities_supported]
  - Mobility Impairments
  - Examples: wheelchair/scooter users, difficulty navigating stairs or long distances, muscular dystrophy, limb loss.
  - Support: accessible buildings and classrooms, seating accommodations, mobility on campus, photocopy assistance for those with limited mobility .
  - Dexterity Impairments
  - Examples: arthritis, repetitive strain injuries, short-term arm or hand injuries.
  - Support: ergonomic keyboards/mice, ability to type exams, assistance with note-taking and materials.
  - Vision Impairments
  - Examples: low vision or total blindness.
  - Support: adaptive technology like screen readers and magnifiers (e.g., JAWS, Kurzweil 3000), alternative-format documents (large-print, digital), orientation aids .
  - Hearing Impairments
  - Examples: partial hearing loss, deafness.
  - Support: captioning services, FM systems, assistive audio devices, note-taking support.
  - Chronic Physical Health Conditions
  - Examples: multiple sclerosis, cancer treatment, severe asthma, chronic fatigue, cardiovascular conditions.
  - Support: flexible deadlines, attendance and exam modifications, rest breaks, accommodation plans tailored to health-related barriers .
  - Temporary Physical Disabilities
  - Examples: recovery from surgery, concussions, fractures or sprains.
  - Support: temporary exam adjustments, classroom seating changes, provision of mobility aids and note-taking resources.

[documentation_requirements]
  - Disability Assessment Form

[documentation_submission]
  - Option 1: Submit through Traditional Registration Option TRO; for students who would like to attend a virtual registration appointment with a student accommodation facilitator.
  - Option 2: Submit through Express Registration Option ERO; for students who do not require a registration appointment with a student accommodation facilitator.

[registration_process]
  - Obtain your disability documentation
  - Submit your Intake form & Documentation - (There are two options for registering with AAS - the Traditional Registration Option (TRO) and the Express Registration Option (ERO).
  - Each option has it's own different online intake form that you complete.
  - TRO is best suited for students who would like to attend a virtual registration appointment with a Student Accommodation Facilitator to ask questions and discuss concerns, and ERO offers students a remote and expedited way for AAS to develop and approve an academic accommodation plan.
  - Monitor your inbox for next steps (Depending on the registration option you chose, you will receive a specific email outlining your next steps.
  - TRO intake form - We'll email you about booking your registration appointment.
  - ERO intake form - We'll email you with information about your accommodation plan.)

[osap_eligibility]
  - Ontario Resident: You must be an Ontario resident. This generally means Ontario is the most recent province where you lived for at least 12 consecutive months, not including time spent as a full-time post-secondary student.
  - Canadian Citizen, Permanent Resident, or Protected Person: You must be one of these to be eligible for OSAP. International students are not eligible.
  - Financial Need: OSAP assesses your financial need based on your educational costs and your available financial resources (including your income, family income, and any other scholarships or savings).
  - Good Standing on Previous Loans: Any previous OSAP loans must be in good standing, with no defaults or restrictions.
