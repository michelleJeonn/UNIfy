Wilfrid Laurier University
==========================

[physical_disabilities_supported]
  - Mobility impairments
  - Wheelchair/scooter use, difficulty walking or climbing stairs, long-distance mobility issues, temporary injuries
  - Dexterity limitations
  - Conditions like arthritis, repetitive strain injuries, hand/arm injuries
  - Vision impairments
  - Low vision or legal blindness
  - Hearing impairments
  - From partial hearing loss to deafness
  - Chronic health conditions
  - Including MS, cancer, asthma, chronic fatigue, cardiovascular issues
  - Temporary physical conditions
  - Post-surgery recovery, concussions, fractures, etc.

[mental_disabilities_supported]
  - anxiety disorders (Generalized Anxiety Disorder, Social Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder)
  - mood disorders (Major Depressive Disorder, Bipolar Disorder), thought disorders (Schizophrenia)
  - eating disorders
  - personality disorders
  - learning disabilities
  - attention disorders (ADD/ADHD)

[physical_accommodations]
  - Extra time, breaks, and private rooms for exams
  - Alternate formats: audio, large print, Braille, accessible PDFs
  - Assistive tech and adaptive software support
  - Note-taking support from peer volunteers
  - One-on-one accommodation planning with advisors
  - Online system to manage accommodations each term
  - Orientation program for incoming students with disabilities
  - Support for physical and temporary conditions
  - Help with accessible housing and campus navigation
  - Confidential and personalized service

[mental_accommodations]
  - Extra time, breaks, and private rooms for exams
  - Alternate formats: audio, large print, accessible PDFs
  - Assistive tech and adaptive software support
  - Note-taking support from peer volunteers
  - One-on-one accommodation planning with advisors
  - Online system to manage accommodations each term
  - Orientation program for incoming students with disabilities
  - Support for learning, mental health, and temporary conditions
  - Confidential and personalized service

[documentation_requirements]
  - Attention Disability: Attention Disability/Autism Spectrum Disorder Verification Form, Psycho-educational assessment (completed using Adult Measures)
  - Brain Injury: Brain Injury Disability Verification Form, Current medical assessment including SCATS, dated within the past six months
  - Autism Spectrum Disorder: Attention Disability/Autism Spectrum Disorder Verification Form, Psycho-educational assessment (completed using Adult Measures), Neuro-psychological assessment (completed using Adult Measures)
  - Hearing Disability: Hearing Disability Verification Form, Current audiologist assessment (completed within past year)
  - Learning Disability: Learning Disability Documentation Requirements PDF, Psycho-educational assessment (completed using Adult Measures)
  - Mental Disability: Mental Disability Verification Form
  - Mental Health/Psychiatric Disability: Mental Health Disability Verification Form
  - Physical/Functional Disability: Physical/Functional Disability Verification Form
  - Vision Disability: Vision Disability Verification Form, Ophthalmologist’s report, Physician assessment, CNIB card/registration number

[documentation_submission]
  - Review Disability Documentation Requirements.
  - If applicable, review Interim Accommodations, UW Double Degree, or Service Animals.
  - Complete the Accessible Learning Registration Form.
  - Submit your documentation.
  - Promptly respond to messages from Accessible Learning in your myLaurier inbox about next steps.

[registration_process]
  - Registration and Documentation - (Review Disability Documentation Requirements - If applicable, review Interim Accommodations, UW Double Degree, or Service Animals. - Complete the Accessible Learning Registration Form - Submit your documentation. - Promptly respond to messages from Accessible Learning in your myLaurier inbox about next steps.)
  - Meet Your Accessible Learning Consultant - (If Accessible Learning determines you are eligible for support after reviewing your registration form and documentation, we will email your @mylaurier email address about meeting with an Accessible Learning consultant.)

[osap_eligibility]
  - To be generally eligible for OSAP, you must:
  - Be an Ontario resident who is a Canadian citizen, permanent resident of Canada, or a protected person. International students are not eligible for OSAP.
  - Be registered in an OSAP-approved program.
  - Be enrolled in an eligible course load.
  - Have previous OSAP loans in good standing, with no defaults or restrictions.
  - Maintain satisfactory academic progress throughout your studies.
  - Demonstrate financial need.
