[physical_disabilities_supported]
  - Mobility Impairments
  - Examples: wheelchair/scooter users, difficulty navigating stairs or long distances, muscular dystrophy, limb loss.
  - Support: accessible buildings and classrooms, seating accommodations, mobility on campus, photocopy assistance for those with limited mobility .
  - Dexterity Impairments
  - Examples: arthritis, repetitive strain injuries, short-term arm or hand injuries.
  - Support: ergonomic keyboards/mice, ability to type exams, assistance with note-taking and materials.
  - Vision Impairments
  - Examples: low vision or total blindness.
  - Support: adaptive technology like screen readers and magnifiers (e.g., JAWS, Kurzweil 3000), alternative-format documents (large-print, digital), orientation aids .
  - Hearing Impairments
  - Examples: partial hearing loss, deafness.
  - Support: captioning services, FM systems, assistive audio devices, note-taking support.
  - Chronic Physical Health Conditions
  - Examples: multiple sclerosis, cancer treatment, severe asthma, chronic fatigue, cardiovascular conditions.
  - Support: flexible deadlines, attendance and exam modifications, rest breaks, accommodation plans tailored to health-related barriers .
  - Temporary Physical Disabilities
  - Examples: recovery from surgery, concussions, fractures or sprains.
  - Support: temporary exam adjustments, classroom seating changes, provision of mobility aids and note-taking resources.

[osap_eligibility]
  - To apply for full-time OSAP aid, you must:
  - register in full-time studies for the session
  - be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident or have protected person status; international students are ineligible.
  - have Ontario residency, that is, have lived in Ontario for at least 12 consecutive months without attending full-time postsecondary school.
  - register in an OSAP-approved degree program. Most degree programs at York are OSAP approved.
  - be neither in default on a previous OSAP student loan nor on OSAP restriction.
  - maintain satisfactory academic progress while receiving OSAP aid.
  - have demonstrated financial need as calculated by the government. Gross family income as well as student resources and assets are taken into consideration.
