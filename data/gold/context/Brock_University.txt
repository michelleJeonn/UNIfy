Brock University
================

[physical_disabilities_supported]
  - Mobility impairments: wheelchair or scooter use, difficulty walking or climbing stairs, temporary injuries or surgery recovery
  - Dexterity limitations: arthritis, repetitive strain injuries, hand/arm impairments
  - Vision impairments, hearing impairments, chronic medical conditions, and temporary health challenges

[mental_disabilities_supported]
  - anxiety disorders (Generalized Anxiety Disorder, Social Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder)
  - mood disorders (Major Depressive Disorder, Bipolar Disorder), thought disorders (Schizophrenia)
  - eating disorders
  - personality disorders
  - learning disabilities
  - attention disorders (ADD/ADHD)

[physical_accommodations]
  - Personalized accommodation plans based on your needs
  - Exam support: extra time, breaks, quiet/private rooms
  - Assistive tech and adaptive software available
  - Note-taking support and lecture recording options
  - Alternate formats: audio, large-print, accessible PDFs, Braille
  - Support for physical and temporary conditions
  - Interim accommodations are possible pending documentation
  - Campus accessible housing and facilities (ramps, elevators, washrooms)
  - Register via intake meeting and manage accommodations each term online
  - Confidential service—no need to disclose diagnosis to professors

[mental_accommodations]
  - Personalized accommodation plans based on your needs
  - Exam support: extra time, breaks, quiet/private rooms
  - Assistive tech and adaptive software available
  - Note-taking support and lecture recording options
  - Alternate formats: audio, large-print, accessible PDFs
  - Support for learning, mental health, and temporary conditions
  - Interim accommodations are possible pending documentation
  - Register via intake meeting and manage accommodations each term online
  - Confidential service—no need to disclose diagnosis to professors

[documentation_requirements]
  - A completed SAS Medical Documentation Form which is available from the SAS reception in ST400 or by emailing askSAS@brocku.ca.
  - A letter or original functional assessment report from a licensed medical practitioner, qualified in the appropriate specialty area (or a general medical practitioner who has preferably been treating the patient over the last 6 months), which must be current (within the last 6 months).

[documentation_submission]
  - Upload through the SAS Student Registration.

[osap_eligibility]
  - To be eligible for OSAP at Brock University, you must typically be:
  - An Ontario resident: This is usually based on where you have lived for the past 12 consecutive months, excluding time spent as a full-time post-secondary student.
  - A Canadian citizen, Permanent Resident, or Protected Person: International students are not eligible for OSAP.
  - Enrolled in an OSAP-approved program of study: This generally includes programs leading to a degree, diploma, or certificate. Certain non-degree programs or upgrading courses may only be eligible if they are specifically approved by the Ministry (e.g., approved micro-credentials or required upgrading courses for admission into a degree program).
  - Enrolled in an eligible course load: This dictates whether you apply for full-time or part-time OSAP.
  - In good standing with previous OSAP loans: You must not be in default on any past OSAP loans or have any restrictions on your OSAP account.
  - Maintaining satisfactory academic progress: You need to meet the academic requirements to continue receiving funding.
  - Able to demonstrate financial need: Your and your family's (if applicable) financial information will be assessed to determine your level of need.
  - Within the lifetime limit of student loan funding: Generally, this is 340 weeks for most students, with exceptions for doctoral students (400 weeks) and students with disabilities (up to 520 weeks).
